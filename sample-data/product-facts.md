# RouteWise product facts (fallback knowledge when the RAG assistant is not configured)

RouteWise is Brightline Logistics' fleet routing and dispatch platform (fictional product for this demo).

Plans: Starter 49 USD per vehicle per month, up to 10 vehicles, route optimization and driver mobile app. Growth 79 USD per vehicle per month, up to 100 vehicles, adds live customer ETA notifications, proof of delivery photos and the public API. Enterprise: custom pricing for more than 100 vehicles, adds SSO (SAML / OpenID Connect), a dedicated success manager and a 99.95% uptime SLA.
Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Starter and Growth. Non-profit organizations get an additional 20% off.
Free trial: every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with up to 5 vehicles. No credit card is required.
Cancellation: monthly plans can be cancelled any time; annual plans can be refunded pro-rated within the first 30 days only.
Integrations: Shopify, WooCommerce, QuickBooks Online and Salesforce natively. REST API and webhooks on Growth and above. API rate limit 600 requests per minute per account.
Support: Starter email support (1 business day), Growth email and chat (4 business hours), Enterprise 24/7 phone with 1-hour response for critical incidents.
Data: hosted on AWS in Canada (ca-central-1) for Canadian customers and in the US (us-east-1) for others. Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). GPS history kept 13 months.
